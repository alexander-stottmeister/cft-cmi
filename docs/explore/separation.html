Module 7 — The separation constraint

If every corner of a recovery chain is weak, the corners cannot be close together. The bound is eΔk ≥ 2/ε, and the interesting thing about it is that it is never attained: the chain that comes closest still exceeds it, by 2.2% at ε = 0.3 and by two parts in a million at ε = 0.003. The constant 2 is sharp only in the limit.

proved Theorem 11.1 — weak corners must be far apart

For the L→R ordinary-Petz chain and 0 < ε < 2, if ζj ≤ ε for every corner j ≥ k — only the corners to the right of pk are used — then e^Δₖ ≥ 2/ε, and e^Δₙ₋¹ ≥ 1 + 2/ε. The proof is a one-dimensional downward recursion. With γj = coth(Δj/2) − 1 = 2/(eΔj − 1) the exact corner data of Lemma 10.3 give γj ≤ ζj(1 + γj+1/2), and downward induction from γn = 0 reaches the fixed point γj ≤ 2ε/(2−ε).

Lemma 10.3 is what makes this possible: the corner strengths are functions of the modular lengths alone, 1/ζₖ = sinh²(Δₖ/2) coth(Δₖ₊₁/2) + (1/2) sinh Δₖ, so eΔk ≤ 1 + 2/ζk with equality if and only if k = n−1, where Δn = ∞ and the coth is 1.

conjectural The second-order network law, and why the separation matters to it

The conjecture is −log F = ∑j,k ζjζk G(yj − yk) + o(|ζ|²), G = c Ĝ, Ĝ(0) = 1/(12π²). Theorem 11.1 is the part of it that is proved. It is what bounds the off-diagonal terms: all corners weak forces every separation above log(2/ε), so the cross terms are O(ε² supΔ ≥ log(2/ε) |Ĝ|) and distinct junctions add in ζ².

What is not settled: the law itself, the uniformity of its o(|ζ|²) remainder in the separations (needed wherever ζ → 0 and Δ → ∞ together), and everything about Ĝ away from the origin. Positive-definiteness does not make Ĝ pointwise positive; its sign for Δ > 0 is open.

What is exact here, and what is measured

The recursion below is a closed form: given the strengths, one square root per corner gives every Δj exactly, because Lemma 10.3 turns 1/ζj into the quadratic (c+1)E² − (4/ζj + 2c)E + (c−1) = 0 in E = eΔj, with c = 1 + γj+1. Your browser runs exactly that. The five minima it is checked against were computed by the referee's independent script and are read from its output file, with the line they came from under each number.
