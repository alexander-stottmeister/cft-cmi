Phi(zeta) and D(zeta) are nondecreasing in zeta (check_note5_theory.tex Prop. 8.1)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Theorem A: exact recovery error of the free fermion · Card type: result · Knowledge-base id: phi-monotone-in-zeta

Statement

For the free chiral fermion the zero-collar recovery error Phi(zeta) = -log F(omega, omega o beta-bar_s) and the relative entropy D(zeta) of the recovered state are nondecreasing functions of zeta = a s/(a+L) on (0, infinity) (rigor/check_note5_theory.tex prop:mono). Proof: at fixed (L, s) the glued map k_s does not depend on a, so for a_1 < a_2 the normal map on F_r(I_2), I_i = (-a_i, L), restricts to the one on F_r(I_1) (uniqueness of the normal extension, card petz-zero-collar-channel); root fidelity and Araki relative entropy are monotone under restriction to a subalgebra, so Phi(zeta(a_1, L, s)) <= Phi(zeta(a_2, L, s)); every pair zeta_1 < zeta_2 is realised by s > zeta_2, L = 1, a_i = zeta_i/(s - zeta_i); and Phi depends on (a, L, s) only through zeta (Note 5 Lemma 1.1, card mobius-reduction-cross-ratio). Note 5 itself left the monotonicity numerical (proof of cor:collapse).
