The first-order compression defect is a commutator with the vacuum symbol: ddelta = [Q, E_I D_chi E_I] (F1 Lemma 6.1)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Optimality of recovery channels (O8, Phases 3 and 3b) · Card type: result · Knowledge-base id: compression-defect-commutator-form

Statement

With d := E_I D_chi E_I one has ddelta = E_I[P, D_chi]E_I = [Q, d], and on the isometric orbit the first-order defect is delta^(1)(zeta,0,0) = [Q, d - zeta] for anti-self-adjoint zeta = E_D zeta E_D, so the tangent problem is expressed through (Q, E_D) and the single operator d.
