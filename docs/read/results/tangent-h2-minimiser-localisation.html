Hypothesis H2 of the tangent problem: localisation of the minimisers of the second-order programme

Status: conjectural — believed, with partial evidence; not proved. Stated under hypotheses H2, quoted below.

Area: Optimality of recovery channels (O8, Phases 3 and 3b) · Card type: conjecture · Knowledge-base id: tangent-h2-minimiser-localisation

Statement

There are C < infinity and s_0 > 0 such that for 0 < s < s_0 the programme min_F g_Q(Q - Q_rec) has a minimiser (X_s, Z_s) of the scaled-family form for a first-order datum (zeta^(s), N_1^(s), Y_1^(s)) with ||N_1^(s)|| + ||Y_1^(s)|| <= C and g_Q(delta(s) - s delta^(1),(s)) = o(s^2) uniformly in s; the document calls it 'the lower bound in disguise' and does not prove it.

Hypotheses

The statement is made under named hypotheses. The sentences are quoted from the statement above; read the status badge only together with them.
