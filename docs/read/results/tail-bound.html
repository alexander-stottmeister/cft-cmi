Ultraviolet tail bound for the windowed fidelity (shape proved; explicit constants 0.173, 0.88 rest on numerical inputs)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Theorem A: exact recovery error of the free fermion · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: tail-bound

Statement

For the free chiral fermion, with Phi_W the window (sub-compressed) recovery error at modular cutoff kappa_c (rigor/tail_bound.tex). (a) PROVED, the shape, as thm:main now states and proves it (corrected 2026-09-24, tail_bound.tex Correction C1): 0 <= Phi - Phi_W <= Phi_d + Y/(1-Y), with Phi_d := T/(2(1-T)), y = 2 e^{2 Phi_d} sqrt(Phi Phi_d) and Y := e^{Phi_W} y <= e^{Phi} y, provided T < 1 and Y < 1 (the hypotheses of thm:main, tail_bound.tex:458-470). Both hold on the admissible region of cor:rate with the inputs of (b), where T <= 1.75 zeta^2/kappa_c^2 <= 0.0175 and Y <= 0.02; the document checks both in cor:rate <1>2 (l. 524, added 2026-09-24, Correction C3). Until 2026-09-24 the document printed this bound, and its hypothesis, with y in place of Y; that form does not follow from the proof's ingredients (DOCUMENT ERROR note on step <1>5). The Y-form is what it now states and proves (Thm 5.1, thm:main, eq:Psi: Bures-angle triangle inequality through the product state rho'' and the purification lower bound lem:purif), with T = 5 tau + nu <= 2 zeta^2/(3 kappa_c^2) + 10 C_2 zeta^2/kappa_c^3 + (12 + 10 C_1 zeta (1 + kappa_c)) e^{-kappa_c}, where C_2 = 4 pi A_3, C_1 = A_1/(4 pi^2) and A_1, A_3 are finite suprema of explicit one-dimensional integrals of the closed-form occupation (Thm 4.2, thm:tau), and |Sigma - zeta^2/(15 kappa_c^2)| <= C_2 zeta^2 kappa_c^-3 + C_1 zeta (1 + kappa_c) e^{-kappa_c}; hence, whenever Phi <= f zeta^2, Phi - Phi_W <= C zeta^2/kappa_c + C' zeta^2/kappa_c^2 on kappa_c >= max{10, 2 log(1/zeta) + 2 log(1 + kappa_c) + 3} with finite C, C'. (b) EXPLICIT CONSTANTS, via numerical inputs: A_1 <= 2.0 and A_3 <= 0.0374, i.e. C_1 <= 0.051 and C_2 <= 0.47 (card tail-constants-numerical: uncertified double-precision and finite-difference evaluations; A_3 <= 0.036 and C_2 <= 0.46 until 2026-09-24, corrected in tail_bound.tex Correction C10, since the supremum A_3 is the zeta -> 0 limit, about 0.0372), and f = 0.0085 (card phi-below-0085-zeta2-numerical) give 0 <= Phi - Phi_W <= 0.173 zeta^2/kappa_c + 0.88 zeta^2/kappa_c^2 (Cor. 5.2, cor:rate, eq:final). eq:final holds given these inputs, and the document now says so: cor:rate states that eq:final rests on these numerical inputs and on a double-precision evaluation of the corrected chain over the admissible region, not on an analytic argument (l. 510-512, 527), and its step <1>1 is repaired, with s(10) = 1.707 <= 1.75 (corrected 2026-09-24, Corrections C2, C3 and C10; until 2026-09-24 the printed proof used a false step at l. 521 and obtained eq:final by dropping the factors 1 + O(zeta^2), DOCUMENT ERROR note of 2026-09-23). With eq:taunu's T (C_2 <= 0.47) the corrected chain of display (a) (Y in place of y) stays <= 0.9841 of the claimed bound on the admissible region, with supremum 0.98405 at the corner (zeta, kappa_c) = (11 e^{-3.5}, 10) = (0.33217, 10), where kappa_c = kappa_dagger = 10 (refb10_tail_sup_c2.py; 0.98019 with C_2 <= 0.46 before 2026-09-24, refb10_tail_sup.py). The grid of tailcheck_ext.py and refb6_tailcheck_corrected.py (zeta step 5e-4) reaches only zeta = 0.3325 there and gives 0.98365 (refb10_tail_sup_c2.py, grid scan; 0.97979 with C_2 <= 0.46, refb6_tailcheck_corrected.py). The pre-correction chain (y in place of Y, as printed until 2026-09-24) gives 0.98343 at the corner (refb10_tail_sup_c2.py); with C_2 <= 0.46 it gave 0.97957 at the corner (refb10_tail_sup.py) and 0.97917 on the grid (tailcheck.py, tailcheck_ext.py). The input f = 0.0085 could be replaced by the proved f <= 0.008516 of rate-of-remainder prop:rrr (2 sqrt(0.875 x 0.008516) = 0.1726 <= 0.173; corrected chain <= 0.9847 of the bound, 0.98467 at the corner, refb10_tail_sup_c2.py; 0.98080 with C_2 <= 0.46). tail_bound.tex now cites prop:rrr, in Correction C3 (l. 935-940, 964-967): as the rigorous basis of f = 0.0085 on (0, 0.8868], the rest of (0, 1] being numerical as on card phi-below-0085-zeta2-numerical, and as a possible replacement of f; cor:rate itself still takes f = 0.0085 (l. 510). Until 2026-09-24 tail_bound.tex did not cite prop:rrr. Supersedes the heuristic tail of Note 5 Prop. 4.1; the zeta^2 log^-2 remainder claimed there was refuted (card rate-of-remainder).
