S10's circle model: the ultraviolet taper suppresses theta by ~20%, and there is no taper-free limit

Status: numerical — measured, with an error bar and a convergence order; not a theorem.

Area: Optimality of recovery channels (O8, Phases 3 and 3b) · Card type: finding · Knowledge-base id: circle-model-taper-suppresses-theta

Statement

Mechanism of the 0.300 vs 0.384 discrepancy. (1) In the NS-circle model of S10 Sec 7 the smoothstep taper on the spectral derivative (window uv in units of L; the Nyquist and the grid's second Fermi point both sit at |nu|/L = 0.5) damps exactly the ultraviolet modes near the moving endpoint where G_* lives. At L=256, a=2, Lg=1: theta = 0.27762, 0.28958, 0.36178, 0.72688, 0.54681 for uv = (0.20,0.32), (0.30,0.42), (0.35,0.47), (0.42,0.49), (0.46,0.499), and 0.17175 with no taper. The sweep is NOT a one-parameter scan: the first three windows share a transition width 0.12, and at that fixed width theta rises monotonically as the taper is relaxed at every L in the file (L=512: 0.28986, 0.29511, 0.32313; L=768: 0.29569, 0.29898, 0.31573); the last two have widths 0.07 and 0.039, and their theta = 0.72688, 0.54681 are artifacts of the sharp transition, not taper-strength data (REF-P0-TAPER). (2) There is NO taper-free limit: without the taper the periodic grid's second Fermi point (nu = +-L/2) doubles the Fermi sea and |u|^2 with it, 0.00739 -> 0.014889 (ratio 2.016), reproducibly in L (0.014889, 0.014912, 0.014921 at L = 256, 512, 768) with theta stuck at 0.17175, 0.19652, 0.20822: the untapered model is STABLE, it simply solves a different problem. Moving the taper's upper edge onto the second Fermi point at fixed transition width does the same (uv = (0.38,0.50): |u|^2 = 0.024005, theta = 0.56798). The |u|^2 = 0.02731 and 0.62691 of the two narrow windows are a separate, transition-sharpness (Gibbs) artifact, NOT the second Fermi point: at the same upper edge with a wider transition the norm is healthy again (uv = (0.25,0.49): 0.007628, theta = 0.36040; uv = (0.09,0.49): 0.007404, theta = 0.30780) (REF-P0-TAPER controls). So the taper is necessary AND it biases theta downwards; S10's window (0.30,0.42) is the safe one and its theta lies below the taper-free value at every L measured; a wider-transition window that passes the same gate more comfortably, uv = (0.25,0.49) (transition width 0.24, |u|^2 3.3% off against the 4.7% for which S10 discards (0.35,0.47)), already gives theta = 0.36040, the taper-free same-mesh value (no superlative is claimed: (0.09,0.49) has a wider transition still and a better norm, 0.007404, but damps the mid-band and gives 0.30780). (3) Proof that the MESH is not responsible (that the taper is the cause is (1)): running the modular Galerkin functional (exact vacuum symbol in closed form, exact continuum defect kernel, no taper, no cap) on the CIRCLE MODEL'S OWN MESH -- cells whose edges are the xi-midpoints of theta_j = 2 pi j / L mapped by xi = log((x+a)/(Lg-x)), x = tan(theta_j/2) -- gives theta = 0.34058, 0.35830, 0.36904, 0.37534, 0.37893 at L = 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, i.e. the Galerkin values, at the same |D| and the same geometry where the tapered circle model gives 0.291, 0.290, 0.295, with |D| = 31, 63, 127, 255 on both sides; and the endpoint cap is not doing the work, since extending it to xi_max = 8.81, 20.81, 260.81 moves theta 0.35830 -> 0.35922 -> 0.35938 -> 0.35938 and saturates (f3_mimic_deep.out). (4) Consequently S10's Richardson in 1/L is also misapplied: the tapered theta_circ rises linearly in log L (theta = 0.28958 -> 0.30173 from L = 256 to 1024; d theta/d log L is flat at 0.0095-0.0096 over L = 512..1024, 0.0094 over 384..1024, and 0.0088 across the quoted endpoints), so continuing that trend to 0.384 would need xi_max ~ 14.8, L ~ 5e6 -- which is a reductio, since a law linear in log L is unbounded and cannot converge to 0.384 at all. S10's proofs P1-P5 and the qualitative conclusions DRAWN FROM theta > 0 ((S) fails, the compression is not the second-order minimiser, E^(2) <= (1-theta) f2 z^2) stand and are strengthened: 1 - theta = 0.616 +- 0.003 instead of 0.70. What does NOT stand is S10's own N2, 'ultraviolet-taper independent', and its verdict box's stability 'in L ... in the ultraviolet taper' (sdp_dual_certificate.tex l.861-863, l.911-913); and N3's non-perturbative 0.705 is a consistency check of the tapered model, not a measurement of 1 - theta.
