Exact single-observable fidelity bound (Alberti-Uhlmann)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Optimality of recovery channels (O8, Phases 3 and 3b) · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: single-observable-bound

Statement

For normal states phi, psi and T = T*: F <= 1 - (eps/2) Delta + (eps^2/2) psi((T-m)^2)/(1 - eps N), hence -log F >= Delta^2/(8V')(1 - Delta N/V'). With one-particle T = dGamma(t): for EVERY recovery channel -log F >= g_Q(Q - Q^beta)(1 - o(1)) in Legendre form. This is a lower bound; it says nothing about the upper half.
