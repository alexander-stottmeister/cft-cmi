Type-III second-variation lemma (fidelity half)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Foundations: CMI as a Markov defect (Notes 1-3) · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: second-variation-lemma

Statement

For every von Neumann algebra with a cyclic separating vector, the fidelity along a differentiable state curve has the second-order expansion with the Bures (SLD) metric; proved in structured form (33 pp). The Kubo-Mori half is only available on unitary orbits (see km-exact-identity).
