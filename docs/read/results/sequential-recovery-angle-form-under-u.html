Angle form of the sequential recovery bound, conditional on Hypothesis (U) (vacuous: (U) is refuted)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof. Conditional. The statement is not unconditional: it rests on U, quoted below. Read the badge only together with them.

Area: Lattice, thermal, massive and gapped geometries (O7, O9, O10) · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: sequential-recovery-angle-form-under-u

Statement

UNDER Hypothesis (U) (card hypothesis-u-natural-cone-fidelity, which is REFUTED): in the setting of card sequential-recovery-bound-type-iii, the Bures angles A = arccos F add along the chain, A(omega|N_m, omega|N_1 o R) <= sum_k A(omega|N_{k+1}, omega|N_k o R_k) (Thm 12.5, thm:w6), and Phi_tot <= -log cos(sum_k arccos e^{-Phi_k}) whenever sum_k arccos e^{-Phi_k} < pi/2 (Cor 12.7(1), eq:w6-master). (U) enters only at thm:w6 <1>4 (l. 1846): all mu_j get natural-cone representatives in one Hilbert space whose inner products are the fidelities, so the spherical triangle inequality (lem:spherical) applies. Since (U) is false, this proof establishes nothing; the implication is recorded so that the knowledge base shows it as vacuous. The unconditional angle inequality is expected to hold (Uhlmann's supremum formula F = sup |<xi, u' eta>|, a sequential choice of vector representatives and lem:spherical), but no project document proves it, and no card uses it.
