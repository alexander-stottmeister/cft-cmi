Three faces of the Bures form (type III)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Foundations: CMI as a Markov defect (Notes 1-3) · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: bures-three-faces

Statement

For a von Neumann algebra M with cyclic separating vector Omega and any a in H with Im<Omega,a> = 0, putting phi(K) = -2 Im<a, K Omega>: (1/4) g_B(a) = (1/2) ||(1-J)(1+Delta)^{-1/2} a||^2 = dist(a, closure of M'_sa Omega)^2 = sup over K in M_sa of [phi(K) - Var_omega(K)], with no domain hypothesis on a.
