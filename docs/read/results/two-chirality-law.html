Two-chirality law for 2D CFTs

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Theorem A: exact recovery error of the free fermion · Card type: result · Knowledge-base id: two-chirality-law

Statement

For a 2D CFT the rotated Petz maps give -log F^(lambda) = Phi(z(1+e^{-pi lambda})) + Phi(z(1+e^{pi lambda})), even in lambda with minimum at lambda = 0 (the ordinary Petz map is best within the rotated family for two chiralities; matches Vardhan-Wei-Zou). For a single chirality the family is monotone and the compression endpoint is best.

How to verify

Note 5 eq. (5.4) and Cor. 5.1 (reworded: optimal within the rotated family only).
