KKT sign criterion: when the isometric orbit carries the all-channel second-order optimum

Status: proved — a complete structured proof. Conditional. The statement is not unconditional: it rests on H1, H2, H3, quoted below. Read the badge only together with them.

Area: Optimality of recovery channels (O8, Phases 3 and 3b) · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: kkt-noise-sign-criterion

Statement

PROVED (rigor/exact_optimum_tangent_problem.tex, Sec. 4). Let delta_* be the b-orthogonal residual of ddelta on the closure of the LINEAR class L = {[Q,G] : G bounded anti-self-adjoint, supported in D} (no minimiser is posited), t_* := SLD(delta_*) (S_Q(t_*) = delta_*, t_*~_ik = w_ik delta_*~_ik), tau_* := E_D t_* E_D and M_* := (1/2) E_D (t_* Q + Q t_*) E_D = Herm(t_{*,DA} Q_AD) + Herm(t_{*,DD} Q_DD) = Herm((t_* Q)_DD). Since w is unbounded, t_* need NOT be Hilbert-Schmidt: M_* and tau_* are quadratic FORMS, defined intrinsically by <psi,M_* psi> := 4 b(delta_*, A(0,|psi><psi|,0)) and <psi,tau_* psi> := -4 b(delta_*, A(0,0,|psi><psi|)) on the domain D of psi for which those directions lie in the form domain F (Def. def:forms); 'M_* >= 0' is a form inequality and lambda_min may be -inf. Normalisation (Lemma lem:norm): g_Q(delta) = (1/4) Tr(t_delta delta), V(t_delta) = 2 g_Q(delta), d g_Q(delta)[eta] = (1/2) Tr(t_delta eta) (S10 (C4) is internally inconsistent with its own S_Q(t) = delta; the ratio and all sign conditions are unaffected). (i) Lemma lem:deriv: the one-sided derivative along an admissible non-isometric direction A(0,N_1,Y_1) is (1/2)[Tr(N_1 M_*) - Tr(Y_1 tau_*)]; it vanishes along the linear class; along the admissible unbounded generator G = -lambda D_chi|_D it equals 2 lambda (1-theta) g_Q(ddelta) (>= 0; > 0 iff theta < 1). (ii) Thm. thm:kktmain: (a) M_* >= 0 and M_* - tau_* >= 0 <=> (c) Tr(N_1 M_*) >= Tr[(N_1^{1/2} tau_* N_1^{1/2})_+] for all bounded N_1 >= 0; (b) M_* >= (tau_*)_+ is sufficient but strictly stronger (the operator order is not a lattice); AMENDED after the referee report: (d) (zeta_*,0,0) is a GLOBAL minimiser of the tangent programme <=> (a) AND (e), where (e) E(zeta) := 2 b(delta_*, A(zeta,0,0)) >= 0 for every admissible isometry generator zeta with A(zeta,0,0) in F; (a) alone is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT, because the feasible cone also contains the one-sided boundary-moving isometry directions (unbounded generators). If (a) and (e) hold then T = (1-theta) g_Q(ddelta), i.e. kappa_opt = 1 - theta exactly, and under H1 and H2 (Thm. thm:value) E^(2) = (1-theta) f_2 z^2 (1+o(1)). If (a) fails, a violating psi gives a strictly better feasible datum (Cor. cor:better, eq:gain2); kappa_opt < 1 - theta needs one admissible psi with Delta < 0 AND finite g_Q(d_psi), so a violation that vanishes with the weight cap or the mesh is a regularisation artefact. (iii) Prop. prop:bdryfun: E MUST be evaluated on the blocks of A (A_AD = -Q_AD zeta, A_DD = -(zeta* Q_DD + Q_DD zeta)); the commutator form -2 b(delta_*,[Q,zeta]) of Lemma lem:commQ is valid only for BOUNDED anti-self-adjoint zeta (where E = 0) and deletes exactly the boundary term (2'). E is additive, positively homogeneous and vanishes on the closure of L; when the cyclicity Tr(t_*[Q,zeta]) = Tr([t_*,Q] zeta) is legitimate (zeta bounded, t_* trace class), E(zeta) = (1/2)Tr(E_D[Q,t_*]E_D zeta) = 0 by the stationarity Prop. prop:S: E is exactly the cyclicity anomaly, a pure BOUNDARY functional, equal to (1/2)Tr((-2 sigma_zeta) M_*) whenever -2 sigma_zeta lies in the form domain of M_*. In the modular frame D = {y>0} has two endpoints (y = 0, the junction with A; y = +inf, the moving endpoint x = 1), and modulo the closure of L the VECTOR-FIELD subcone of admissible generators is generated by zeta_0 = -E_D d/dy E_D (the shift semigroup g(y) -> g(y-a), i.e. x -> 1 - e^{-a}(1-x); moves the junction y = 0) and zeta_inf = -lambda D_chi|_D (further compression; moves y = +inf); for the whole admissible cone this is hypothesis H3. E(zeta_inf) = 2 lambda (1-theta) g_Q(ddelta) is proved, hence E(zeta_inf) >= 0; E(zeta_inf) > 0 is equivalent to theta < 1, which is NOT proved (rem:whytheta (i), :1461-1464; verdict gap :1504-1507). So, ON THE MODEL CONE {alpha zeta_0 + beta zeta_inf + closure of L : alpha, beta >= 0}, (e) is equivalent to the single inequality E(zeta_0) = 2 b(delta_*, A(zeta_0,0,0)) >= 0; the identity E(zeta_0) = (1/2) Tr((-2 sigma_{zeta_0}) M_*) would need -2 sigma_{zeta_0} = delta_0 (x) delta_0 in the form domain of M_*, which fails (delta_0 is not in h_D). HYPOTHESIS H3 (cone hypothesis, Def. hyp:H3, read in F): for every admissible isometry generator zeta with A(zeta,0,0) in F there are alpha, beta >= 0 and eta in the b-closure of the linear class with A(zeta,0,0) = alpha A(zeta_0,0,0) + beta A(zeta_inf,0,0) + eta. (No boundary-form reformulation: that would be strictly weaker, trivially true for zeta = i m(y), and would not imply (e).) The reduction to the two model directions is PROVED ONLY for generators of vector-field type; shift semigroups of multiplicity > 1 (Cooper-Wold) and unbounded skew-adjoint zeta = i m(y) with [Q, i m] outside the closure of L escape both arguments, and excluding them is exactly the content of H3. Under H3, if A(zeta_0,0,0) is not in F then alpha = 0 is forced and (e) holds OUTRIGHT, E(zeta) = beta E(zeta_inf) >= 0 (rem:H3space), the junction inequality never being invoked; that A(zeta_0,0,0) is not in F (E(zeta_0) = +inf, so (e) is vacuous at zeta_0 rather than satisfied with a margin) rests on F2's divergent corner term (rem:T5 (ii)), i.e. it is numerical. E(zeta_0) >= 0 remains the operative test for REGULARISED junction directions (finite mesh), which is what F2 T5 measures. (iv) Prop. prop:S: stationarity on the LINEAR class <=> E_D[Q,t_*]E_D = 0, i.e. (t_* Q)_DD = M_* self-adjoint: S10's condition (S) holds automatically at the new point. (v) Prop. prop:s10 (trace-class t; S10 Thm 3.2 and Prop 5.2), dictionary to S10 Thm 3.2: Sigma_2 -> (tQ)_DD is the loss-of-isometry (N_1) condition M >= 0; Sigma_1 = Sigma_2 - t_DD >= 0 is the NOISE (Y_1) condition M >= tau; Sigma_1 = Sigma_1* is the rotation condition. NUMERICAL, NOT PROVED HERE (recorded on kkt-noise-criterion-numerics, all-channel-optimum-value, theta-value and compression-not-second-order-minimiser): (a) holds in the limit, although every finite run fails it (F2 T4, rigid frame of Prop 6.2: lambda_min(M_*)/||M_*|| = -4.1e-3, -1.9e-3, -8.2e-4, -3.7e-4 at h = 0.24, 0.12, 0.06, 0.03; violation ~ h^1.15 and ~ 1/Ymax, rank-one gain ~ h^1.85); E(zeta_0) > 0 on the model cone (F2 T5: E(zeta_0)/g_Q(ddelta) = +34.1, +66.9, +131.3, +258.0 at h = 0.24, 0.12, 0.06, 0.03 for the admissible backward/dissipative scheme, a scheme-dependent total ~ h^-0.97 dominated by an upwind-viscosity term; centred = 0, the linear class; forward = inadmissible, negative; the continuum piece is the corner term (1/2) M_*(0,0)/h = +10.3, +16.1, +24.7, +37.2, ~ h^-0.60, so M_*(y,y) -> +inf as y -> 0+ and E(zeta_0) = +inf: the junction direction is infinitely penalised at first order); E(zeta_inf) reproduces 2(1-theta_h) g_Q(ddelta) to 1e-15, and the A-form and boundary-form expressions agree to 2e-10 ... 2e-8; the commutator form returns, even for zeta_inf, -9.1e-6, +1.9e-4, -9.9e-3, +7.4e-2 at h = 0.24, 0.12, 0.06, 0.03 and -63, -1.6e6 on other rungs of the scan (O(1), no information on the true 2 lambda (1-theta) g_Q(ddelta) = 1.26-1.37 g_Q(ddelta); prop:bdryfun (2')); theta = 0.384 +- 0.003, hence kappa_opt = 1 - theta = 0.616 +- 0.003 conditional on H1, H2 AND H3 and on the mesh-verified (a) and (e); and (S) FAILS at the compression, i.e. E_D u E_D != 0 (||E_D u E_D||_2^2/||u||_2^2 = 0.0143, S10 cor:maximal eq:thetalb).

Hypotheses

This result is conditional: at least part of the statement holds only where the hypotheses below hold. The sentences are quoted from the statement above; read the status badge only together with them.

Hypothesis labels used: H1, H2, H3.

If (a) and (e) hold then T = (1-theta) g_Q(ddelta), i.e. kappa_opt = 1 - theta exactly, and under H1 and H2 (Thm. thm:value) E^(2) = (1-theta) f_2 z^2 (1+o(1)).
