g_B = 2c/(3 pi^2) for the compression direction of every diffeomorphism-covariant net (corollary of Theorem B)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof. Stated under hypotheses H1, H2, H3, quoted below.

Area: Theorem B: universality for diffeomorphism-covariant nets · Card type: constant · Knowledge-base id: const-gb-general-nets

Statement

For every diffeomorphism-covariant net on S^1 of central charge c satisfying the standing hypotheses of Theorem B (separable, CDIT axioms, Bisognano-Wichmann, Haag duality, Reeh-Schlieder), the Bures form of the zero-collar compression direction is g_B = 4 dist(T(g_tot) Omega, closure(A(I)'_sa Omega))^2 = 2c/(3 pi^2), so -log F = (s^2/8) g_B + o(s^2). This is the value statement of Theorem B (card thm-b-universality), whose hypotheses (H1)-(H3) hold for every such net (card implementer-hypotheses); the constant is fixed by c-linearity and the free-fermion value at c = 1 (card const-gb).
