A-C restriction bound: (Tcond) implies E_AC = 0 exactly; applied on the hopping chain (4,8,2), (4,12,2), (4,16,2)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Optimality of recovery channels (O8, Phases 3 and 3b) · Card type: result · Knowledge-base id: ac-lower-bound-zero

Statement

Proved, as an implication (Prop. prop:AC-zero, rigor/optimality_second_order.tex:408-434): if some T: h_C -> h_B satisfies (eq:Tcond) Q_AB T = Q_AC, T*T <= 1, T* Q_BB T <= Q_CC <= 1 - T*(1 - Q_BB) T, then X* := (0 T) and Z := (1/2) 1_B (+) Q_CC give (X, Z) in F whose recovered symbol on h_A (+) h_C is the vacuum one, so a gauge-invariant quasi-free channel beta in C reproduces omega exactly on A(A) v A(C): E_AC = 0 and the restriction bound (eq:AC-bound) of Lemma lem:restriction gives nothing. Application: the hypothesis is established only numerically, on the half-filled hopping chain for (L_A,L_B,L_C) = (4,8,2), (4,12,2), (4,16,2), where T = Q_AB^+ Q_AC satisfies (Tcond) (card ac-intertwiner-exists-hopping-chain); on these three chains E_AC = 0 exactly, consistent with the direct convex minimisation of numerics/optimality/lower_bound.py (lb.log: E_AC = 1.53e-9, 9.83e-10, 6.99e-9, solver floor, against -log F(omega_AC, omega_A (x) omega_C) = 3.17e-3, 1.66e-3, 1.03e-3). Nothing is claimed for the continuum or for other geometries: lb.log's third row (6,12,3) gives E_AC = 2.27e-5 (E_AC/z^2 = 0.0045), and at (6,12,3) and (8,16,4) the Moore-Penrose T violates (Tcond) (margins -0.133, -0.464; numerics/kb-checks-2026-09-23/tcond_check_extra.out). The tex's conclusion that the O8 route ('bound the Bures distance of the vacuum A-C state to the B-mediated ones') yields no bound at all (:450-457) extrapolates from these three chains by a heuristic mechanism and is not part of this card.
