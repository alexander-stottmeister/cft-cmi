Exact relative-entropy identity on unitary orbits (bounded generators; affiliated generators with Omega in D(A))

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Kubo-Mori (relative entropy) coefficient s2 · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: km-exact-identity

Statement

(a) Bounded generators (thm:exact, rigor/kubo_mori_second_variation.tex:215): for A = A* in M (bounded), u_s = e^{isA}, omega_s = omega o Ad u_s: D(omega || omega_s) = || h(Delta)^{1/2} (u_s - 1) Omega ||^2 in [0, infinity], h(lambda) = lambda - 1 - log lambda, with the sum rule int (lambda - 1) d nu_s = 0 along the orbit; liminf s^{-2} D >= g_KM/2 with no further hypothesis (thm:lower), g_KM = -2 <A Omega, log Delta A Omega> (lem:kms); D = (s^2/2) g_KM + o(s^2) under (U_eps) (thm:main), which holds in particular when A is modularly analytic in a strip, (A_eps) => (U_eps) (lem:AtoU). (b) Affiliated generators (prop:unbounded, l. 774-778): for A = A* affiliated with M and Omega in D(A), the exact identity, the sum rule, lem:kms and the lower bound hold verbatim, and the second-order expansion holds under (U_eps); for unbounded A the modular-analyticity route (A_eps) => (U_eps) is not available (lem:AtoU is for bounded A). (c) Not applicable to the recovery curve of Note 7, whose generator T(g_tot) is affiliated with the global algebra but not with the interval algebra, so e^{isA} is not in M (Gap QK2, l. 822).
