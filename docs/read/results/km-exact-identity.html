Exact relative-entropy identity on unitary orbits

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Kubo-Mori (relative entropy) coefficient s2 · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: km-exact-identity

Statement

For omega_s = omega(e^{-isA} . e^{isA}) with A affiliated with the algebra: D(omega || omega_s) = || h(Delta)^{1/2} (e^{isA} - 1) Omega ||^2, h(lambda) = lambda - 1 - log lambda. Gives the lower bound unconditionally and the full second-order expansion under modular analyticity. Not applicable to the recovery curve, whose generator is not affiliated with the interval algebra.
