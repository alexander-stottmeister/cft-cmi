The Petz map is not second-order optimal (compression 4x better)

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Optimality of recovery channels (O8, Phases 3 and 3b) · Card type: result · Knowledge-base id: petz-not-optimal

Statement

Per chirality the zero-collar compression gives Phi(z) = f2 z^2 + O(z^3) against Phi(2z) = 4 f2 z^2 for the ordinary Petz map; the range constraint beta(A(BC)) subset A(B) is active at the compression. For a 2D CFT: 2 f2 z^2 against 8 f2 z^2. Lattice best/Petz = 0.27-0.28.
