Longo-Xu CMI of adjacent intervals

Status: proved — a complete structured proof.

Area: Foundations: CMI as a Markov defect (Notes 1-3) · Card type: theorem · Knowledge-base id: longo-xu-cmi

Statement

For adjacent intervals A=(-a,0), B=(0,b), C=(b,b+c) in the vacuum of a chiral net of central charge c (c = r for r complex fermions), I(A:C|B) = (c/6) log[(a+b)(b+c)/(b(a+b+c))]. Read as the quantum-Markov defect of the chain A-B-C; the Petz recovery error is the natural quantitative companion.

How to verify
