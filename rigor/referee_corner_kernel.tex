\section{Item 1: definitions, the shift sign, the box corner 2}
\textbf{(a) The quantity.} \texttt{corner\_kernel.bil} returns $\tfrac14{\rm Re}\sum_{ij}\overline{A_{ij}}B_{ij}/W_{ij}$ with
$A,B$ in $Q$'s eigenbasis (\texttt{to\_eig}) and $W_{ij}=w_i(1-w_j)+w_j(1-w_i)$ (\texttt{sld}): the real polarisation of
$g_Q$ of the brief. The polarisation identity holds to $\le2.2\times10^{-16}G_0$ in every T0 rung (\texttt{kernel\_t0.out})
and qfi\_c2 polarised equals it to $\le2.3\times10^{-16}$ where $Q_{\rm box}$ is resolved (\texttt{kernel\_box.out}). OK.

\textbf{(b) Sign of the shift.} \texttt{t0\_defect} calls \texttt{f3\_t0.ddot(e - e[k], k)} with $e_k=\Delta$: the cell
integrals of \texttt{f3\_t0.kern} at $(y_1-\Delta,y_2-\Delta)$ over cells left/right of node $k$, i.e.\ the kernel is
supported on $y_1<\Delta<y_2$ --- the corner sits at $y=+\Delta$, the translate of lem:field's field
$\zeta w(y-y_p)\mathbf 1_{y>y_p}\partial_y$ with $y_p=\Delta$ (\texttt{rigor/network\_corner\_calculus.tex}:399--412). In
$y=-\log(1-x)$ this is $x_p=1-e^{-\Delta}>0$, the same side as the box's $p'(\Delta)>0$. In the continuum the sign is
immaterial ($G(-\Delta)=g_Q(\delta_{-\Delta},\delta_0)=G(\Delta)$ by translation invariance of $Q$ and symmetry of the real
form); on the meshes it decides which window edge the second corner approaches: $Y_{\max}-\Delta\ge12$ (T0) and
$\Lambda/2-\Delta\ge22$ (box). OK.

\textbf{(c) $p'(\Delta)$.} With $y_I(x)=\log\frac{x+a}{L-x}$ (\texttt{rigor/phase6\_brief.md}, modular frame):
$y_I(p')-y_I(0)=\Delta\iff L(p'+a)=a e^{\Delta}(L-p')\iff p'=\frac{aL(e^{\Delta}-1)}{L+ae^{\Delta}}$, exactly. OK.

\textbf{(d) $\zeta'=\zeta$.} The second corner is the corner $x'=0$ of the frame $(a',L')=(a+p',L-p')$ of the SAME interval
$I$, with the `first' kernel of parameter $s'$, i.e.\ $\sigma'=s'/L'$ (Theorem A's $\sigma=s/L$, phase6\_brief). By the
definition $\zeta=\kappa\beta_I(p)$, $\kappa=\sigma$: $\zeta'=\frac{s'}{L'}\cdot\frac{(p'+a)(L-p')}{a+L}=\frac{s'a'}{a+L}$, which
equals $\zeta$ exactly for G3's choice $s'=\zeta(a+L)/a'$. The card's statement is right (an identity, not only the
30-digit check of K0(e)); lem:density (:364--372) is not needed for it.

\textbf{(e) Both frames discretise the same $G$.} For $I=(-a,L)$, $r=a/L$, $t=\xi-\xi_0$:
\lstep{1}{1}{$x=\frac{a(e^t-1)}{1+re^t}$, $\beta_I(x)=\frac{a(1+r)e^t}{(1+re^t)^2}$.}
\lby{$x=(Le^{\xi}-a)/(1+e^{\xi})$ with $e^{\xi}=re^t$; $x+a=\frac{a(1+r)e^t}{1+re^t}$, $L-x=\frac{a+L}{1+re^t}$ (as $Lr=a$)}
\lstep{1}{2}{With $u=-x_1$, $v=x_2$: $\frac{uv}{(u+v)^2}=\frac{(1-e^{t_1})(e^{t_2}-1)(1+re^{t_1})(1+re^{t_2})}{(1+r)^2(e^{t_2}-e^{t_1})^2}$.}
\lby{<1>1; $u+v=\frac{a(1+r)(e^{t_2}-e^{t_1})}{(1+re^{t_1})(1+re^{t_2})}$ after expanding the numerator}
\lstep{1}{3}{$\sqrt{\beta_I(x_1)\beta_I(x_2)}\,\sigma\frac{uv}{(u+v)^2}=\zeta\,{\rm kern}_{T0}(t_1,t_2)$,
${\rm kern}_{T0}(t_1,t_2)=-\frac{\sinh(t_1/2)\sinh(t_2/2)}{\sinh^2((t_1-t_2)/2)}$, $\zeta=\sigma\frac{aL}{a+L}$.}
\lby{<1>1, <1>2: the factors $(1+re^{t})$ cancel, $\sigma a/(1+r)=\sigma aL/(a+L)=\zeta$; then $1-e^{t_1}=-2e^{t_1/2}\sinh\frac{t_1}2$,
$e^{t_2}-1=2e^{t_2/2}\sinh\frac{t_2}2$, $e^{t_2}-e^{t_1}=2e^{(t_1+t_2)/2}\sinh\frac{t_2-t_1}2$}
\lqed{1}{4}\ So the box `first' defect in half-density form is exactly $\zeta\,\dot\delta(\xi-\xi_p)$ at each corner, with
$\zeta=1/3$ for both (by (d)); dividing by $\zeta_1\zeta_2=1/9$ (code: \texttt{/zeta**2}) gives the same $G(\Delta)$ as the
T0 frame. The conversions (C1)--(C5) of \texttt{KERNEL\_RESULTS.md} are right; the box line symbol
$q(\kappa)=(1+e^{\kappa})^{-1}$ on $e^{ik\xi}$, $\kappa=2\pi k$, is $1-w(k)$ of the T0 convention, and $W$ is invariant under
$w\to1-w$, so $g_Q$ is the same.
\begin{verdictok}
Item 1: definitions, sign of the shift, $p'(\Delta)$, $\zeta'=\zeta$ and the frame conversions are correct; (c), (d), (e)
hold exactly (proved above), not only to 30 digits.
\end{verdictok}
