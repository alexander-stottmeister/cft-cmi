\section{Item 1: model and dictionary}\label{sec:i1}
\paragraph{Step map.} \texttt{run\_protocol} (lattice\_chain.py l.~97--126) applies, for every step, the
Gaussian form of the vacuum Petz map of the step's own inclusion $\cF(A_B)\subset\cF(A_B\cup A_{\rm new})$:
$X_M=T_M^{a}(T_B^{-a}\oplus1_{\rm new})$, $a=\tfrac12-\tfrac{i\lambda}2$ (code: \texttt{pw(...,0.5,-lam/2)},
\texttt{pw(...,-0.5,lam/2)}), with $T_M,T_B$ the vacuum blocks $G=C/(1-C)$ of $M=A_B\cup A_{\rm new}$ and of
$A_B$; $X$ is the identity off $M$; $T\leftarrow X(T\oplus1_{\rm new})X^\dagger$; $\log K\leftarrow\log K-\log\det(1+T_M)+\log\det(1+T_B)$.
Derivation (my own): for number-conserving Gaussian operators $e^{c^\dagger Xc}e^{c^\dagger Yc}=e^{c^\dagger Zc}$
with $e^Z=e^Xe^Y$ (Klich; quoted in the docstring of numerics/lattice/petz\_lattice.py), so
$\rho_M^{a}\rho_B^{-a}\,x\,\rho_B^{-\bar a}\rho_M^{\bar a}$ with $x=Ke^{c^\dagger Xc}\otimes1_{\rm new}$ has matrix
$X_M(T\oplus1)X_M^\dagger$ (since $(T_B^{-a})^\dagger=T_B^{-\bar a}$) and prefactor
$K K_M^{a+\bar a}K_B^{-(a+\bar a)}=KK_M/K_B$ with $K_Y=\det(1+T_Y)^{-1}$, $a+\bar a=1$. This is VWZ (5.1)
verbatim (p.~43: ``$\mathcal P_{R\to RS}(\cdot)=\rho_{RS}^{\frac12-\frac{i\lambda}2}\rho_R^{-\frac12+\frac{i\lambda}2}(\cdot)
\rho_R^{-\frac12-\frac{i\lambda}2}\rho_{RS}^{\frac12+\frac{i\lambda}2}$ (5.1) where $\rho_R,\rho_{RS}$ are the reduced
density matrices of the target state''), applied to ``whatever state the earlier steps produced'' (the state $T$
carried from step to step), as network\_corner\_calculus.tex (C3)(i) prescribes. The fidelity is the root fidelity
$F=\lVert\sqrt\rho\sqrt\sigma\rVert_1$ of VWZ (2.1) (p.~7): \texttt{fid\_relent} computes
$-\log F=\frac12\log\det(1+G_1)+\frac12\log\det(1+T_2)-\sum\log(1+\sqrt{\tau})$, $\tau=\mathrm{eig}(G_1^{1/2}T_2G_1^{1/2})$,
which I re-derived from $\sqrt{\rho_1}\rho_2\sqrt{\rho_1}\propto e^{c^\dagger Yc}$, $e^{Y}=T_1^{1/2}T_2T_1^{1/2}$,
$\Tr e^{c^\dagger Yc/2}=\det(1+e^{Y/2})$; and $D=-S_1+\sum_l[\log(1+s_l)-Y_{ll}\log s_l]$ with
$Y=V_2^\dagger CV_2$, which is $-S_1-\Tr\rho_1\log\rho_2$ for $\log\rho_2=-\sum\log(1+s_l)+c^\dagger\log T_2\,c$.
\begin{verdictok}
The code implements the brief's model: per step the vacuum (rotated) Petz map of the step's own inclusion, applied
to the state produced so far, in the exact Gaussian composition rule of petz\_lattice.py; root fidelity as in VWZ
(2.1) and paper~1; the per-step identity $K\det(1+T)=1$ is a genuine consequence of trace preservation.
\end{verdictok}
