# Phase 5, track F1 (analytic) — summary, rev. 5 (referees 1-3, F2 T4/T5, F3) (2026-09-15) — `rigor/exact_optimum_tangent_problem.tex`, 31 pp since the corrections of 2026-09-24 (27 pp at rev. 5), compiles clean

1. **Chart of F (exact).** `(X,Z)∈F ⟺ N:=1-XX* ≥ 0 and 0 ≤ Y:=Z-XQ_BB X* ≤ N`; `X=(1-N)^{1/2}V` with the *polar* V a co-isometry **iff 1∉σ_p(N)** (sharp; *some* co-isometric factor exists iff `dim ker(1-N) ≤ dim ker X`), `V*=WU`, U an isometry of h_D; all defect blocks exact.
2. **Tangent problem.** `δ^(1)=δ̇+A(ζ,N_1,Y_1)` (as forms on C_c^∞; H1 upgrades it to the g_Q-norm), `A_AD=-Q_ADζ+½Q_AD N_1`, `A_DD=-(ζ*Q_DD+Q_DDζ)+½{N_1,Q_DD}-Y_1`; `δ̇=[Q,d]`, zero AA block (locality) and zero DD block (Möbius rigidity). Each datum gives *exactly* feasible channels at finite s ⇒ upper bound (given H1).
3. **Boundary-moving isometries (Thm 3.11, amended).** Bounded generators give nothing beyond S10's class (σ=0). For unbounded ones the absorption identity gives only an **inclusion** of cones, whose right-hand side is described by *how G arises* — G is the anti-symmetric part `σ-ζ*` of an admissible ζ (sign corrected, correction C1 below); that G itself generates an isometry semigroup is **not** claimed (it would need `C_c^∞(D)` to be a core for ζ). Reversing it is false and fatal — `+λD_χ|_D` is anti-symmetric but generates an outward flow, and admitting it would give `T=0` (Rem 4.8); σ is the *boundary form* `⟨σg,g⟩=Re⟨ζ*g,g⟩ ≤ 0` (Lumer–Phillips, C_0-semigroup); the singular boundary N_1 are not in the g_Q-closure of the bounded ones.
4. **KKT criterion (Thm 4.4, amended).** `τ_*=E_D t_* E_D`, `M_*=½E_D(t_*Q+Qt_*)E_D`, as **quadratic forms** via `b(δ_*,·)` on a domain 𝔇 (t_* need not be Hilbert–Schmidt; λ_min may be -∞). Derivative `=½[Tr(N_1M_*)-Tr(Y_1τ_*)]`; **(a) M_*≥0 and M_*≥τ_* ⟺ (c)**; (b) `M_*≥(τ_*)_+` sufficient, stronger. **(a) is necessary but NOT sufficient**: global optimality is **(a) and (e)**, with
   **(e) `E(ζ) := 2b(δ_*, A(ζ,0,0)) ≥ 0`** for every admissible isometry generator ζ — evaluated on the **blocks** `A_AD=-Q_ADζ`, `A_DD=-(ζ*Q_DD+Q_DDζ)`; the commutator form `-2b(δ_*,[Q,ζ])` is valid **only** for bounded anti-self-adjoint ζ (where E=0) and is blind to the boundary form (F2 T5: even for ζ_∞ it carries no information about E — not merely solver noise but O(1) and worse on the finer scans, Prop 4.10(2′); correction C4 below). E vanishes on the linear class and is exactly the **cyclicity anomaly** (Prop 4.3 kills `Tr([t_*,Q]ζ)`), hence a pure **boundary** functional, `=½Tr((-2σ_ζ)M_*)` whenever `-2σ_ζ` lies in the form domain of M_* (it does not at ζ_0: `-2σ_{ζ_0}=δ_0⊗δ_0`, Prop 4.10(5); correction C10 below). Modulo the linear class the **vector-field subcone** of admissible generators (not, as far as is proved, the whole cone; correction C6 below) is generated by `ζ_0=-E_D∂_yE_D` (shift semigroup `g(y)↦g(y-a)`, i.e. `x↦1-e^{-a}(1-x)`; junction y=0) and `ζ_∞=-λD_χ|_D` (further compression; moving endpoint y=+∞). **Proved: `E(ζ_∞)=2λ(1-θ)g_Q(δ̇)≥0`** (`>0` iff θ<1, which is not proved; correction C2 below). The reduction of (e) to these two directions is proved **only for vector-field generators**; multiplicity > 1 shift semigroups (Cooper–Wold) and unbounded skew-adjoint `ζ=i·m(y)` outside `L̄` are admissible and escape both arguments, so the reduction of (e) to the two model directions is **Hypothesis H3** (Def 4.12, Rem 4.13, Rem 4.14). **H3 is exactly eq. (33)**: for every admissible ζ with `A(ζ,0,0) ∈ 𝔉` there are α,β ≥ 0, η ∈ L̄ with `A(ζ,0,0) = α·A(ζ_0,0,0) + β·A(ζ_∞,0,0) + η`, **read in 𝔉** — no boundary-form reformulation (that would be strictly weaker, trivially true for `ζ=i·m(y)`, and would not imply (e)). If `A(ζ_0,0,0) ∉ 𝔉` (numerical, not proved: F2's divergent corner term, Rem 4.11(ii); correction C3 below), (33) forces **α = 0**: H3 says every feasible admissible generator is, mod L̄, a non-negative multiple of ζ_∞, so **(e) holds outright under H3** via `E(ζ_∞)≥0`, and the junction test is never invoked. The content of H3 is the *exclusion* of the two non-vector-field families, not a scalar test; `E(ζ_0) ≥ 0` remains the operative test for **regularised** junction directions (finite mesh), which is what F2 T5 measures. E takes values in [-∞,+∞]: `E(ζ):=+∞` when `A(ζ,0,0)∉𝔉`, and such ζ are not feasible data — (e) is then *vacuous*, not satisfied (this is the case at ζ_0).
5. **Consistency with S10.** Stationarity on the *linear* class forces `(t_*Q)_DD=M_*` self-adjoint = condition (S) at the new point; `Σ_2→(tQ)_DD` is the N_1 condition, `Σ_1=Σ_2-t_DD≥0` the **noise** condition, `Σ_1=Σ_1*` the rotation condition. S10 (C4) is internally inconsistent with its own `S_Q(t)=δ`; correct relations `Tr(tδ)=4g_Q`, `V(t)=2g_Q` (our Lemma 1.1).
6. **θ.** Geometry-free Wiener–Hopf problem in the modular frame (`y=-log(1-x)`, `Q=(1+e^{-2πp})^{-1}`, `w(y)=-4sinh²(y/2)1_{y>0}`, kernel `K=sinh²(πΔ)/[cosh(πΔ)(cosh(πΣ)+cosh(πΔ))]`). **Numerical input (F3): θ = 0.384 ± 0.003, 1-θ = 0.616 ± 0.003**; S10's 0.300 is a taper-suppressed lower bound; `3/π²`, `1/3`, `1-2/π` all **excluded**. `θ<1` unproved (only the converse). Closed form still open.

**Numerical status.** (a) holds only as an **extrapolation**: every finite run fails (a) (F2 T4, rigid frame: `λ_min(M_*)/‖M_*‖ = -4.1e-3 … -3.7e-4` at h=0.24…0.03), and the violation extrapolates to 0⁻ (~h^1.15 and ~1/Y_max; rank-one gain ~h^1.85 → artefact; correction C8 below). (e) holds **on the model cone** (F2 T5). Decomposition `E^disc(ζ_0) = ½[M_*(0,0) + h·Tr((-Δ)M_*) + M_*(Y_max,Y_max)]`: the tabulated total `+34.1, +66.9, +131.3, +258.0` (h=0.24…0.03) is `~h^{-0.97}`, dominated by the **scheme-dependent upwind-viscosity** term; the corner row `½M_*(0,0)/h = +10.3, +16.1, +24.7, +37.2 ~ h^{-0.60}` **represents** the continuum E(ζ_0); it diverges and is **not a margin** (correction C9 below). Both diverge ⇒ `M_*(y,y)→+∞` as `y→0+`, `A(ζ_0,0,0)∉𝔉`, so (e) is *vacuous* at ζ_0; centred scheme = 0 (linear class), forward (inadmissible) = negative, i.e. the sign is decided by admissibility. `E(ζ_∞)` reproduces `2(1-θ_h)` to 1e-15. **So the numerics are consistent with the isometric orbit carrying the global tangent optimum on the model cone and κ_opt = 1-θ = 0.616 ± 0.003, conditional on H1, H2 and H3** — a global minimiser, not a unique one (Thm 4.4(d); correction C8 below). **Open hypotheses**: **H3** (cone hypothesis — evaluate E on multiplicity > 1 shifts and on unbounded skew-adjoint ζ, or prove the cone reduction; the sharpest remaining analytic task); H1 (g_Q-differentiability; fails catastrophically for the inadmissible contraction family, so admissibility is not a technicality); **H2** — which *is* the lower bound in disguise (existence + scaled form + boundedness + uniformity); coercivity `g_Q≥¼‖·‖₂²` does not localise (X,Z) since Q_AB has 0 in its essential spectrum. Unconditional substitute: the finite-s weak-duality certificate (needs `Δ_z(t)≥0`).
