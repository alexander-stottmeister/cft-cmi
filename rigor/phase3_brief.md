## Track S8 (O8): second-order optimality of the Petz map
Setting (free chiral fermion first, general nets second): vacuum omega on A(ABC), adjacent intervals A, B, C. A recovery channel acts on B alone:
in the Heisenberg picture alpha = id_A (x) beta with beta: A(BC) -> A(B) normal unital completely positive (for fermions: even, graded-tensor
compatible); the recovered state is omega^beta := omega|_{A(AB)} o (id (x) beta). The Petz map with reference omega|_{A(BC)} and its rotations are
particular beta's; the zero-collar geometric compression is the t -> infinity member (Notes 4-5). Optimal recovery error:
    E_opt := inf_beta [ -log F(omega, omega^beta) ]  over all admissible beta.
Known: Petz value -log F = f_2 zeta^2 + ..., f_2 = c/(12 pi^2); single-chirality geometric compression: f_2/4 (Note 5 Cor 5.1); the CMI bound
-2 log F <= I = (c/6) zeta + ... is loose by a power of zeta; no lower bound on E_opt beyond 0 is known.
Structure to exploit: omega^beta restricted to A(BC) equals omega|_{A(B)} o beta|_{A(BC)}, which can reproduce omega|_{A(BC)} exactly (Petz does); the
A-C correlations of omega^beta are omega(a beta(c)) with beta(c) in A(B): the recovered A-C correlations are A-B correlations of the vacuum evaluated
on the image of beta. The vacuum's A-C connected correlations that are NOT of this form are what any recovery misses. Second order: the tangent
vectors of the recovered states form a convex set K_rec of functionals on A(ABC); E_opt at second order = (1/8) x the Bures-distance^2 from the
vacuum's own tangent (zero) ... more precisely the recovered state at zero collar is O(zeta) away from omega, and E_opt = min over beta of the
Bures form of (omega^beta - omega) to second order in zeta. Tasks: (1) formalise E_opt and prove elementary properties (convexity of the set of
recovered states; the infimum is attained or approached by even quasi-free beta for the free fermion? decide); (2) FREE FERMION: restrict to quasi-free
(Gaussian) channels beta, which are parametrised at the one-particle level by a contraction T: L^2(BC) -> L^2(B) and a noise term (Gaussian channel
covariance rule Q_out = T^* Q_in T + N, N >= 0 constrained by complete positivity: 0 <= Q_out <= 1 for all admissible inputs); compute the minimal
second-order recovery error over this class numerically in the modular frame (Note 5 pipeline: box compression, spectral window, quasi-free fidelity
formula) as a convex or semidefinite optimisation on the one-particle level; compare with f_2 (Petz), f_2/4 (compression), and report the optimal T;
(3) LOWER BOUND for all channels: use that F(omega, omega^beta) <= F of the restrictions to any subalgebra; choose the subalgebra generated by A(A) and
a single mode of A(C) (or A(A) and A(C) with A(B) traced out): the recovered AC correlations must factor through B, i.e. the recovered state on A(A) v A(C)
is omega_{AB} o (id (x) beta|_{A(C)}) with beta(A(C)) subset A(B); bound the fidelity between omega|_{AC} and any state of this form by a data-processing
argument on the SLD metric or by the Bures distance between the vacuum AC two-point function and the set of "B-mediated" AC two-point functions
{<a beta(c)>}: for quasi-free states this is a one-particle problem (the AC block of the vacuum symbol Q_{AC} versus Q_{AB} T for contractions T);
(4) decide whether the Petz map is optimal at second order; if not, exhibit the better channel and its coefficient. Deliverable: rigor/optimality_second_order.tex
+ numerics/optimality/*.py, honest verdict boxes.
